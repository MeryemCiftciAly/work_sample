Data and Storytelling

The Lina Christmas Project

A narrative-driven analytics project presented as a chronological timeline. The story follows an analytically minded 11-year-old who designs a small revenue-generating initiative to purchase and ship holiday gifts independently.

The project walks through the complete analytical lifecycle—from dataset design and data generation, to SQL querying, validation, and insight development—culminating in structured, question-driven conclusions.

Instead of traditional dialogue, the character’s responses are grounded in query results, demonstrating how analytical methods can anchor storytelling in structured, verifiable data.

NYC Waste Collection Analysis

A project analyzing public data from the New York City Sanitation Department to explore how waste is categorized across the five boroughs.

This timeline demonstrates the full analytical process: obtaining and cleaning raw data, performing SQL queries to examine trends, validating categorization accuracy, and visualizing insights in a presentation.

The analysis identifies the most prevalent waste categories per borough and provides insights into collection practices, highlighting patterns in proper and improper waste categorization.
