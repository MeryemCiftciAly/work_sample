The Lina Christmas Project

A unique storytelling project that uses the Christmas holiday as a creative framework. I crafted a narrative centered on 11-year-old Lena, exploring her world through a creative lens.

The Data-Driven Narrative: To bring the story to life, I built custom datasets within the narrative structure. On Christmas Eve, when Lena’s family asks her questions about her project, I used SQL to query the data and find the exact answers.

Instead of traditional dialogue, Lina’s responses are data-driven insights—demonstrating how technical skills can be used to anchor creative storytelling in factual accuracy.
