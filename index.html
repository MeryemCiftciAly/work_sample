About

I am a data analyst with a strong foundation in business operations and administrative support. My background allows me to approach data with a practical, decision-oriented mindset, ensuring insights are accurate, relevant, and actionable.

I began my career supporting operational and compliance-driven teams, where accuracy, documentation, and trust in data were critical. Over time, I transitioned into analytics to work more directly with data—cleaning it, analyzing it, and translating it into insights that inform strategy and execution.

I bring a balanced perspective that combines technical analytics skills with real-world business context. I am particularly effective in environments where data supports leadership decisions, operational improvement, and cross-functional collaboration.

Most Recent Project: Lina’s Christmas Project (SQL & Data Modeling)

This project was designed as an end-to-end analytical case study, using a narrative framework to model a real-world financial and operational problem involving an 11-year-old entrepreneur.

I designed a relational dataset representing sales, shipping, and expenses. Using SQL, I queried the data to answer business-style questions related to profitability, cost allocation, and resource trade-offs.

Skills Used: SQL (Intermediate), Data Modeling, Relational Tables, Data Cleaning, Business Logic.
